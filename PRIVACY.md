# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 1, 2025

mindmatters ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application (the "Service").

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We prioritize your privacy. We do not collect, store, use, or share any personal data or non-personal data from our users.

No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data.

No Tracking: We do not track your usage of the app or your browsing history.

No Device Data: We do not access your device's storage, camera, microphone, or contacts list without your explicit action (and even then, data is processed locally on your device and never sent to us).

## 2. Third-Party Services
Since we do not collect any data, we do not share any data with third-party vendors, service providers, or advertising networks.

No Advertising: This app does not display third-party advertisements that track user behavior.

No Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics services (like Google Analytics or Firebase) to track user interaction.

## 3. Children’s Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. As we do not collect personal information from any users, we explicitly do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

## 4. Data Retention
Because we do not collect any data, there is no data retention policy applicable. Your usage of the app remains entirely on your device.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
